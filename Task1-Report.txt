1. Executive Summary
This report analyzes customer transaction and behavioral data for Alfido Tech to identify purchasing patterns, customer segments, retention trends, and churn risks. Data cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, and customer segmentation techniques were applied using Python and machine learning methods.
The analysis identified valuable customer groups, inactive customers at risk of churn, and seasonal sales patterns. Based on the findings, several recommendations have been proposed to improve customer engagement, retention, and overall business performance.

2. Objective of the Analysis
The primary objectives of this project were:
Perform data cleaning and feature engineering
Analyze customer purchasing behavior

7. Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis)
Customers were segmented using RFM analysis based on:
Recency: Days since last purchase
Frequency: Number of purchases
Monetary: Total spending amount
K-Means clustering was applied to group customers into four segments.
Segment Profiles
Segment 0 – Loyal Customers
Frequent purchases
High revenue contribution
Low churn risk
Segment 1 – High-Value Customers
Highest spending behavior
Important for long-term profitability
Segment 2 – At-Risk Customers
Long inactivity periods
Low recent engagement
High churn probability
Segment 3 – New or Occasional Customers
Low purchase frequency
Potential for future growth

8. Retention & Churn Analysis
Customer retention trends were analyzed using monthly active customers and recency metrics.
Findings
Some customers showed high inactivity periods
Churn risk increased significantly after long gaps in purchasing
Active customer counts varied across months
The analysis suggests the need for retention-focused engagement strategies.

9. Key Findings
A small percentage of customers contribute a large share of revenue.
High-recency customers are more likely to churn and require re-engagement efforts.
Seasonal purchase patterns indicate opportunities for targeted marketing campaigns.
Loyal customers demonstrate strong repeat purchasing behavior.
Product demand varies significantly across categories.

10. Recommendations for Alfido Tech

1. Introduce a Loyalty Program
Reward repeat customers using points, discounts, or cashback offers to improve retention.
2. Launch Personalized Marketing Campaigns
Use customer purchase history to send personalized recommendations and targeted promotions.
3. Re-engage Inactive Customers
Offer discounts and reminder campaigns to customers with high recency scores.
4. Focus on High-Performing Products
Increase promotions and inventory planning for products with consistently high sales.
5. Improve Customer Retention Strategies
Provide subscription benefits, referral programs, and customer support improvements to strengthen engagement.
