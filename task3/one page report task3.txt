Introduction

Web traffic logs were reviewed to evaluate website usage trends, traffic source information, landing page efficiency, and other related aspects to increase website engagement and conversion rates. Data cleaning was followed by exploratory data analysis to make valuable findings.

Executive Summary

The purpose of this study was to analyze website traffic logs and investigate the traffic sources, behavior, and activity metrics of the website's visitors. Website traffic logs were processed to find metrics such as sessions, users, referral sources, landing pages, and actions. The visuals were generated for traffic analysis, geographical location patterns, and user engagement behaviors.

It was discovered that there are various traffic referral sources and different geographical locations contributing to the traffic data, but few channels contribute to most of the website traffic data. Specific landing pages on the website receive more traffic compared to others. The behavior of users also indicates that those who use more pages tend to engage more on the website.

Analysis of the traffic geography indicates that certain countries and even specific cities have many active users, which may be useful in future targeted marketing campaigns. In addition, traffic referral analysis indicates different levels of user engagement, meaning that some channels provide better traffic than others.

In summary, it can be seen that the site enjoys positive visitor behavior overall; however, there is scope for enhancing aspects related to visitor retention, lowering the bounce rate, and increasing conversions.

Three Actionable Insights
1. Most Visited Pages Make the Biggest Difference in Increasing Engagement
->Enhancing pages that experience the highest levels of traffic will help to maximize retention and session lengths.
2. Traffic Sources Play a Role in the Possibility of Conversions
->Pages with traffic sources with low bounce rates and higher time spent need more investment than other traffic sources.
3. Certain Pages Act as an Obstacle for Visitors
->Users tend to leave from certain pages; therefore, the quality of those pages can be improved for better conversions.

Five optimizations to improve conversions

1.Optimize High-Traffic Landing Pages
->Optimize the load time of your most visited landing pages, making sure the content is clear and easy to understand with clear calls to action.
2.Optimize Your Website's Navigation
->Create related page and recommended page links to allow users to explore more pages on your website.
3.Low Bounce Rate Through Content Personalization
->Make sure the content on your most visited pages reflects user intent based on the traffic source and its intent.
4.Enhance Your Calls to Actions
-?Ensure you have visible, compelling calls to actions on your important pages.
5.Focus On High-Performing Traffic Sources
->Direct your marketing spend towards traffic sources that bring more engaged traffic and conversion possibilities while minimizing your spend on low-performing traffic sources.

Results

1. Most of the traffic goes to a small number of landing pages
The analysis of traffic distribution reveals that there are relatively few links which generate high traffic volume. These are the entry pages to the website.

2. The traffic is concentrated in several countries and cities
It was revealed that most of the web traffic is concentrated in specific geographical regions, providing an opportunity for launching marketing campaigns targeted at certain geographical areas.

3. There is bounce rate detected at several landing pages
Several landing pages have the bounce rate since each of them generates only one click.
