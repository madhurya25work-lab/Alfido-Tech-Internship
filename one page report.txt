Customer Behavior Analysis Summary – Alfido Tech
Overview:
This summary highlights key findings from an analysis of a customer transaction database aimed at understanding purchasing patterns, segmentation, revenue trends, and churn risks. After data cleaning and feature engineering, RFM-based segmentation was conducted.

Key Findings:
1. Revenue contribution is dominated by loyal customers

According to the RFM results, only some of the customers are responsible for generating most revenues. They tend to make frequent purchases, and their spending level is high.

2. Some customers have high churn probability

Customers with high recency values have not purchased any items for a long period, making them likely churners. Therefore, customer retention measures should be put into place to engage them again.

3. There are differences in purchase trends month-to-month

Monthly sales review demonstrated revenue and customer activity fluctuation throughout the year.

Recommendations:
1. Introduce loyalty programs

Provide discounts, cashbacks, or any rewards that encourage clients to continue buying.

2. Initiate re-engagement campaigns

Reach out to inactive customers using email, offer coupons or make any other attempt to revive them.

3. Maximize revenue by promoting products during profitable months

Conclusion:
The analysis was able to provide valuable insight into customer buying patterns, important customer segments, and even customer churn threats.
